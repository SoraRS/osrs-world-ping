# OSRS World Ping

A small Windows app that helps you find a good Old School RuneScape world for your connection.

See regularly updated ping readings, browse members and free-to-play worlds, and save up to five favorites. Lower ping usually means less network delay. Open the gear to adjust settings or check your connection.

No RuneScape account login is needed. Your favorites and settings stay on your PC.
